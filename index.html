Why I Love Travelling

Born and raised in Singapore, I find it easy for me to slip and get stuck in the Wake Up, Work, Eat, Sleep and Repeat routine. Travel reminds me that there is no one way to live my life. I love to imagine what it would be like if I were born in the places that I travel to.
