9 Amazing Things To Do In Hong Kong

In my time living here in Hong Kong, these are my favourite things to do.

I did little research before coming to Hong Kong for a semester long student exchange programme. In my memory, Hong Kong was merely a land of Dim Sum, Egglets and Mahjong. Of course, I later realised I was devastatingly wrong, because Hong Kong has so much more to offer.

1. Take a stroll along Victoria Harbour

I remember vividly, the Victoria Harbour skyline left me speechless. It was just stunning. When my parents came over to Hong Kong to visit me, it was the first place I brought them to as well. Go and see for yourself.

And while you are at it, be sure to compare hand sizes with celebrities along the Avenue of Stars.

2. Take the Star Ferry

You will notice that there are many different ships or cruises you can take, costing about 40HKD to 60HKD. The Star Ferry however, I believe is the best deal especially for broke students like you and me.

It costs a very affordable amount of 5 HKD and you can get on it by simply tapping your octopus card at the gantry. It is a 5 minute ride to Central on Hong Kong Island and trust me, it would be one of the best 5 minutes during your time in Hong Kong. I strongly encourage you to sit on the second deck, because this way, you could get a better view and also feel the cool breeze on your face.

3. Go on the Peak Tram to Victoria Peak

Must do, the peak tram is the first tram in Hong Kong. Steep. Of course there are other ways as well.

4. Heal your inner child at Hong Kong Disneyland

Wow. I love disneyland.

5. Marvel at the Big Buddha on Lantau Island

Gorgeous.

6. See dried pufferfish at Tai O Fishing Village

Nemo's dear friend puffed up and sun-dried.

7. Hike along Dragon's Back

One of Hong Kong's most iconic hikes.

8. Climb Lion's Rock

Hong Kong's elderly hiking enthusiasts are no joke.

9. Visit Chi Lin Nunneries and Nan Lian Gardens

Architecture.

*Bonus* - Watch a Cantonese Movie

It hits different.
